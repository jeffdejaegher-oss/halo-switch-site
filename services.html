Foundation

The software package. It applies the first-slice Microsoft 365 tenant build in one unattended push. One company tenant or a stack of MSP tenants — same package.

What the package applies

The first-slice build. Identity locked down, licences assigned from groups, and a tenant you can operate the next morning.

  • The first slice

    Break-glass admin accounts, multi-factor authentication for everyone, legacy sign-in protocols closed, Conditional Access rolled out in report-only before it is enforced, licence groups that follow department rather than a spreadsheet, joiner and leaver procedures, mail protection, and backup. Built for Business Premium or E3 — the same build either way.

    One push, after the tenant, admins, and SKUs exist

  • The push

    You create the new Microsoft tenant, create the admin accounts, and order the SKUs. Foundation does the rest unattended. Dry-run first, against the real tenant, with a log of what it would change before anything is written.

    The human front is on purpose. The product is the push.

How the package runs

Product habits. They keep a live tenant from breaking while it is being changed.

  • Rehearsed first

    Every change runs as a dry-run against the tenant and reports exactly what it would do. Nothing is written until that output has been read. Access policy lands in report-only mode first, so you can see who it would have blocked before it blocks anyone.

  • Nobody gets locked out

    Break-glass accounts are created and tested before access policy is switched on, and are deliberately excluded from it. Lockout recovery is a written procedure, not an improvisation at seven in the morning.

  • Licences drive access

    Groups are built from staff attributes — department, role, site — so access and licences follow people as they join, move, and leave. A mixed estate of full and frontline licences is normal and handled.

  • Evidence, not assurances

    The package leaves a record: what was configured, what was deliberately left off, where the recovery keys live, and the runbooks for day-to-day operations. Written to survive the next person who owns the tenant.

Optional consulting

A person on the work, if you want one. Not required to buy or run Foundation.

  • Device management

    Laptops encrypted and enrolled from the box, shared workstations that staff can sign in and out of cleanly, personal phones covered by app protection without taking control of the phone, and a lost device dealt with the hour it goes missing. Existing unmanaged machines get a staged path in rather than a wipe.

    Typical licence: Microsoft Intune, included in Business Premium and E3

  • Security uplift

    For organizations on E5 or moving to it: risk-based sign-in policy, phishing-resistant credentials for administrators, just-in-time privileged access instead of standing admin rights, retention and audit through Purview, and a security monitoring pipeline. Layered onto an existing foundation, not a rebuild.

    Requires Microsoft 365 E5

  • Ongoing care

    A monthly plan for the work that never finishes: onboarding and offboarding staff, watching for configuration drift, reviewing who holds administrative rights, and checking that backups and device compliance are still healthy.

    Monthly, sized to headcount

Google Workspace or mail moves are scoped in a written SOW when they are needed. They are not a Halo Switch product.
