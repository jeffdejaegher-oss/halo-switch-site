What we do

New Microsoft 365 tenant setup and hardening for small, mid, and large companies: identity locked down, devices managed, evidence you can hand to an auditor.

Engagements

Each engagement is one fixed-fee outcome. Most organizations start with the foundation and add from there.

  • Tenant setup

    The core build. Break-glass admin accounts, multi-factor authentication for everyone, legacy sign-in protocols closed, Conditional Access rolled out in report-only before it is enforced, licence groups that follow department rather than a spreadsheet, joiner and leaver procedures, mail protection, and backup. Delivered on Business Premium or E3 — the same build either way.

    Typical: 4–8 weeks, fixed fee

  • Device management

    Laptops encrypted and enrolled from the box, shared workstations that staff can sign in and out of cleanly, personal phones covered by app protection without taking control of the phone, and a lost device dealt with the hour it goes missing. Existing unmanaged machines get a staged path in rather than a wipe.

    Typical licence: Microsoft Intune, included in Business Premium and E3

  • Security uplift

    For organizations on E5 or moving to it: risk-based sign-in policy, phishing-resistant credentials for administrators, just-in-time privileged access instead of standing admin rights, retention and audit through Purview, and a security monitoring pipeline. Layered onto an existing foundation, not a rebuild.

    Requires Microsoft 365 E5

  • Ongoing care

    A monthly plan for the work that never finishes: onboarding and offboarding staff, watching for configuration drift, reviewing who holds administrative rights, checking that backups and device compliance are still healthy, and handling the awkward situations — the travelling clinician, the locum, the compromised mailbox.

    Monthly, sized to headcount

Google Workspace or mail moves are scoped in a written SOW when they are needed. They are not a Halo Switch product.

How the work gets done

The delivery method is the product. These are the habits that keep a live tenant from breaking while it is being changed.

  • Rehearsed first

    Every change runs as a dry-run against your real tenant and reports exactly what it would do. Nothing is written until that output has been read. Access policy lands in report-only mode first, so you can see who it would have blocked before it blocks anyone.

  • Nobody gets locked out

    Break-glass accounts are created and tested before access policy is switched on, and are deliberately excluded from it. Lockout recovery is a written procedure, not an improvisation at seven in the morning.

  • Licences drive access

    Groups are built from staff attributes — department, role, site — so access and licences follow people as they join, move, and leave. A mixed estate of full and frontline licences is normal and handled.

  • Evidence, not assurances

    Each engagement ends with a handover pack: what was configured, what was deliberately left off, where the recovery keys live, and the runbooks for day-to-day operations. It is written to survive you replacing me.
