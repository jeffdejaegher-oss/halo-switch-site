How we work

Fixed-fee. Scripted. No mystery hours. Scopes are built around Google Workspace → Microsoft 365 migration or new tenant configuration — for small, mid, and large organizations.

1. Discovery

Clarify which offer applies: migrating from Google Workspace, or configuring a new Microsoft 365 tenant. Inventory domains, SKUs, admin roles, and license gaps. Gaps are documented before any automation runs in production.

2. Fixed-fee scope

Each engagement is a fixed-fee package around that offer. Delivery capabilities (dynamic groups, lifecycle, license groups, Intune, SSO / Conditional Access readiness) are included only as needed to complete the outcome. Dollar amounts are agreed in the proposal — this site does not list public price cards.

3. Scripted delivery

Work ships through the open Halo Switch scaffolds: license-mapped PowerShell, least-privilege Graph, dry-run / offline-testable paths, then lab before production.

4. Handoff

You keep the runbooks, audits, and documentation. No tenant secrets land in the shared toolkit repository.

No public price list is published here because dollar amounts depend on scope and licenses. Contact Halo Switch for a fixed-fee proposal matched to your migration or new tenant engagement.
