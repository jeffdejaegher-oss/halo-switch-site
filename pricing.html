How we work

Fixed-fee. Scripted. No mystery hours. Scopes follow the licenses you already own and the workstreams in the Halo Switch toolkit.

1. Discovery

Inventory domains, SKUs, admin roles, and license gaps against the workstreams you want. Gaps are documented before any automation runs in production.

2. Fixed-fee scope

Each engagement is framed as a fixed-fee package around specific workstreams (dynamic groups, lifecycle, license groups, Intune, SSO). Dollar amounts are agreed in the proposal for that customer — this site does not list public price cards.

3. Scripted delivery

Work ships through the open Halo Switch scaffolds: license-mapped PowerShell, least-privilege Graph, dry-run / offline-testable paths, then lab before production.

4. Handoff

You keep the runbooks, audits, and documentation. No tenant secrets land in the shared toolkit repository.

No public price list is published here because the project plan does not define fixed dollar amounts. Contact Halo Switch for a scoped fixed-fee proposal matched to your licenses.
