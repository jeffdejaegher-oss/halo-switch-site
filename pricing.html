How we work

Fixed fee, agreed before anything starts. You will know what is being changed, when, and what it costs, and you will see it rehearsed before it is real.

1. Discovery

A short call and a read of the current tenant: how many staff, which Microsoft licences you already own, how devices are handled today, who holds administrative rights, and what your insurer or risk assessment is asking for. Gaps get written down before anything is proposed.

2. A written scope

One fixed-fee outcome, with what is included and what is deliberately out. Business Premium is capped at 300 staff, so if you are near that line the scope says so and quotes the right licence rather than discovering it halfway through.

3. Rehearsed delivery

Changes run as a dry-run first and report what they would do. Sign-in policy goes live in report-only mode so you can see the effect before anyone is blocked. Break-glass accounts exist and are tested before the locks go on.

4. Handover

You get the configuration record, the runbooks for daily operations, where recovery keys live, and a list of what was left switched off and why. No credentials or tenant data leave with me.

5. Ongoing care, if you want it

A monthly plan covering staff joining and leaving, configuration drift, administrative access reviews, backup and device compliance checks, and the awkward days. Optional, month to month, and priced on headcount.

Prices are not published here because they depend on staff count, licences, and the state of what already exists. A discovery call produces a real number, not a range.
